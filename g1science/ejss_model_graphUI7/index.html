About this Simulation

This customisable graph generator empowers educators and students to create, modify, and explore interactive visual representations of data and mathematical functions. Users can adjust parameters in real-time, observe dynamic changes, and download graphs for presentations, worksheets, or digital portfolios.

Learning objectives: Understand relationships between variables through interactive graph manipulation | Develop data interpretation and visualization skills | Create and customize scientific and mathematical representations for assessment and communication
