About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows learners to manipulate physical models in real-time, observe dynamic behavior, and visualize relationships between variables. Students can adjust parameters, run experiments, and export results for further analysis, fostering deeper understanding of complex scientific concepts through active exploration and experimentation.

Learning objectives: Understand cause-and-effect relationships in physical systems through interactive manipulation | Develop data analysis skills by collecting and exporting simulation results | Apply scientific reasoning to predict and verify outcomes in dynamic models
