Electric Field and Charges Simulation

Explore how electric field lines and vectors behave when you place positive and negative charges on the canvas.

  • or to add charges.

  • Toggle Field Vectors or Field Lines to switch between different visualizations.
  • Use Presets to explore common configurations, like a dipole.

Pedagogical Tip: Encourage learners to predict the shape of field lines before toggling them on. Then compare the result and discuss why field lines must start on **positive charges** and end on **negative charges** (or extend to infinity). This fosters **conceptual understanding** of electric fields.
