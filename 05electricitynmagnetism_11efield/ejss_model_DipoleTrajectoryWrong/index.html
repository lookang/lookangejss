About this Simulation

This simulation models electric multipole fields and allows learners to explore how dipoles behave within complex charge configurations. Students can visualise field patterns, observe dipole trajectories, and investigate the forces acting on charged systems to deepen their understanding of electrostatics.

Learning objectives: Understand how electric multipole moments affect field geometry and dipole motion | Analyse the relationship between charge distribution and resultant electric forces | Apply field visualisation to predict and verify dipole behaviour in non-uniform fields
