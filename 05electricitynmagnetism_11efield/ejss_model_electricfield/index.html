About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models electric field lines around point charges and charge configurations. Students can observe how electric field vectors originate from positive charges and terminate at negative charges, visualizing field strength through vector density and direction. The dynamic model helps learners understand field behavior in three dimensions.

Learning objectives: Visualize and describe the pattern of electric field lines around isolated and multiple charges | Understand how electric field strength relates to distance from a charge | Predict field line orientation and density for different charge configurations
