About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows learners to explore how vectors break down into horizontal and vertical components. By manipulating a vector dynamically, students visualize the relationship between a vector's magnitude, direction, and its component parts. Real-time measurements help develop understanding of vector decomposition in two dimensions.

Learning objectives: Understand how vectors resolve into perpendicular components | Measure and compare horizontal and vertical components quantitatively | Apply vector component concepts to real-world scenarios involving forces and motion
