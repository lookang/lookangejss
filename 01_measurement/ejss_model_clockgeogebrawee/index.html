About this Simulation

This interactive clock model allows students to explore how clock hands rotate and represent different times. By manipulating hand positions and observing orientation changes, learners develop a deeper understanding of time measurement, angle relationships, and how analog clocks function as measurement instruments.

Learning objectives: Understand how clock hands move in relation to hours and minutes | Explore the angular measurement and rotation of clock components | Develop skills in reading and interpreting analog time displays
