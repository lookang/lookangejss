About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows learners to explore vector components and understand how individual displacement vectors combine to produce a resultant. By manipulating vector components along the x and y axes, students visualize the relationship between component vectors and their resultant, deepening their understanding of vector addition and measurement in physics.

Learning objectives: Understand how vectors decompose into perpendicular components | Visualize and calculate resultant displacement from component vectors | Apply vector addition principles to real-world measurement scenarios
