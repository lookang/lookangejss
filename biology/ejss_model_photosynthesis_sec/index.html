About this Simulation

This interactive simulation lets students investigate the key factors affecting photosynthesis, including light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration, and temperature. Through hands-on exploration and automatic curve fitting, learners can visualise how these variables influence photosynthetic rate and discover real-world biological relationships dynamically.

Learning objectives: Identify and manipulate variables that affect the rate of photosynthesis | Analyse experimental data and interpret fitted curves to understand enzyme kinetics | Apply quantitative reasoning to biological processes and predict outcomes under different conditions
