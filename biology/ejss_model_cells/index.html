About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows learners to visualize and explore cell structures at a detailed level. Students can observe organelles, cell membranes, and internal cellular components, manipulating views and scales to understand how cells are organized and how their parts work together to support life processes.

Learning objectives: Identify and describe the structure and function of major cell organelles | Understand how cell components work together to perform vital biological functions | Develop observational skills using interactive visualization tools
