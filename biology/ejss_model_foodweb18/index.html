About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models the food web of Sungei Buloh, a biodiverse wetland ecosystem. Learners can visualize how different organisms interact through feeding relationships, observe energy transfer between trophic levels, and explore how changes in animal populations affect ecosystem balance and stability.

Learning objectives: Understand how organisms are connected through feeding relationships in an ecosystem | Identify producers, consumers, and decomposers within a food web | Investigate the effects of population changes on ecosystem dynamics and food web stability
