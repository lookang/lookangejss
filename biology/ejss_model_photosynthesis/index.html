About this Simulation

This interactive lab simulates the key factors affecting photosynthesis — light intensity and CO₂ concentration. Observe how the rate of oxygen bubble production changes as you adjust the variables, mimicking a real Elodea (water plant) experiment.

Learning objectives: Understand how light intensity, CO₂ concentration, and temperature affect the rate of photosynthesis in Primary School Biology.
