About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models the Bohr model of the atom, allowing learners to visualise electron orbits and discrete energy levels. Students can observe how electrons transition between energy levels, emit or absorb photons, and explore the quantised nature of atomic energy through hands-on manipulation of the model.

Learning objectives: Understand the concept of quantised energy levels in atoms | Visualise electron transitions and relate them to photon emission or absorption | Apply the Bohr model to explain atomic structure and energy quantisation


Title and author:
