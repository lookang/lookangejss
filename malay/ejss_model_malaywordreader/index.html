About this Simulation

This interactive Malay word reader simulation enables learners to explore Malay vocabulary through dynamic word positioning and interactive engagement. Students can visualise word placement on a coordinate system, interact with individual words to trigger feedback, and develop reading fluency by engaging with multiple word elements simultaneously in a customisable visual environment.

Learning objectives: Recognise and read Malay words accurately in an interactive context | Understand spatial word organisation and improve reading fluency through repeated interaction | Develop vocabulary recognition skills and build confidence in Malay language comprehension
