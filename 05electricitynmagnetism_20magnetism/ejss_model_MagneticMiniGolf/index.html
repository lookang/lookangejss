About this Simulation

This interactive mini golf game combines magnetism with physics by allowing learners to hit a golf ball toward a hole while magnetic forces influence its trajectory. Students can adjust variables like ball size, hole size, and starting position to explore how magnetic fields affect motion, making abstract concepts tangible through gameplay.

Learning objectives: Understand how magnetic forces affect moving objects | Investigate relationships between object properties and magnetic interaction | Apply physics concepts strategically to solve problems
