## ✅ DESCRIPTION

```
Visualise the magnetic field of a bar magnet and watch a compass needle respond
to it — in real time. This is a step-by-step tutorial of an interactive Easy JS
Simulation (EJSS) that shows the dipole field, compass behaviour, Earth's field,
and the superposition of two magnets, all from the underlying physics.

Try it yourself (free, runs in browser):
👉 https://iwant2study.org/lookangejss/05electricitynmagnetism_20magnetism/ejss_model_MagneticBarFieldrotationwee/

WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
─────────────────
• How field vectors map the dipole field around a bar magnet
• Why field strength falls as 1/r³ (and what that looks like)
• How a compass equilibrates and oscillates as a damped harmonic oscillator
• How Earth's geomagnetic field combines with a local magnet
• How two interacting magnets produce attraction, repulsion and neutral points

CHAPTERS
─────────
00:00  Intro — what the simulation shows
00:24  Field vectors and the 1/r³ law
00:57  Rotate the magnet (dropdown presets)
01:34  Drag and rotate to any angle
02:15  Show the compass — probe the field at any point
03:01  Compass oscillation (damped SHM)
03:46  Add Earth's magnetic field
04:29  Two magnets: attraction, repulsion and neutral points
05:13  Recap and how to explore on your own

KEY PHYSICS
───────────
Dipole field magnitude:
  • Axial (end-on):       B = (μ₀/4π) · 2m / r³
  • Equatorial (broadside): B = (μ₀/4π) · m / r³
  • General:              B = (μ₀ m / 4π r³) · √(1 + 3cos²φ)

Compass needle (damped SHM):
  dω/dt = −k|B|·sin(θ − θ_eq) − bω
  where θ_eq = atan2(By, Bx)
```
