About this Simulation

This interactive Ferris wheel simulation models uniform circular motion and rotational dynamics. Learners can visualize how riders experience centripetal acceleration, observe the relationship between angular and linear velocity, and investigate how radius and rotation rate affect forces in circular systems.

Learning objectives: Understand centripetal force and acceleration in circular motion | Relate angular velocity to linear velocity and radius | Analyze normal forces and apparent weight on rotating systems
