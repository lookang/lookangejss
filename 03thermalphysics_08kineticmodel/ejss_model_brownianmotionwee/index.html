About this Simulation

This simulation models Brownian motion in a gas, allowing learners to observe how particles move randomly due to collisions with surrounding molecules. Students can visualize the relationship between particle behavior, temperature, and kinetic energy while exploring fundamental concepts of the kinetic model of matter in real time.

Learning objectives: Understand how random molecular collisions cause visible Brownian motion | Investigate the relationship between temperature and particle velocity in gases | Apply kinetic theory concepts to explain observable particle behavior
