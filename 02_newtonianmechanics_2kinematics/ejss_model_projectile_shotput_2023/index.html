About this Simulation

The Frog Game models projectile motion through an engaging shot put scenario. Learners adjust launch angles and initial velocities to predict and observe how projectiles travel through space, experiencing firsthand how initial conditions affect range, height, and flight time in real-world motion contexts.

Learning objectives: Understand how launch angle and velocity influence projectile range and trajectory | Analyze the independent horizontal and vertical components of projectile motion | Apply kinematic equations to optimize shot put distance and predict landing positions
