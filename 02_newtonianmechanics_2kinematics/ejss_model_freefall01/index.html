About this Simulation

This free fall simulator allows students to observe and analyse the motion of objects falling under gravitational influence. Learners can manipulate initial conditions, observe real-time position and velocity changes, and visualise displacement-time and velocity-time graphs simultaneously to deepen their understanding of kinematic relationships.

Learning objectives: Understand how gravity produces constant acceleration in free fall | Relate graphical representations to physical motion and predict future motion states | Apply kinematic equations to solve problems involving freely falling objects
