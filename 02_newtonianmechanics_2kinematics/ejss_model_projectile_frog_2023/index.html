About this Simulation

The Frog Game is an interactive kinematics simulation where learners launch a frog across a pond by adjusting initial velocity and launch angle. Students explore how these variables affect projectile trajectory, range, and flight time in a game-based learning environment.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between launch angle and projectile range | Analyse how initial velocity impacts motion trajectories | Apply kinematic principles to predict and optimise projectile motion
