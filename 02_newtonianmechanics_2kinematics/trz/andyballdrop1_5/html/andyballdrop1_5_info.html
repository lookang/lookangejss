Tracker Experiment

Students will be able to deduce from distance - time graphs
(i) at rest
(ii) moving with uniform velocity
(iii) moving with non-uniform velocity


Students will be able to deduce from the shape of a velocity-time graph when a body is:
(i) at rest
(ii) moving with uniform velocity
(iii) moving with uniform acceleration
(iv) moving with non-uniform acceleration
