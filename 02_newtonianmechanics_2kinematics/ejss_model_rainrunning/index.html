About this Simulation

This interactive simulation investigates the classic question: is it better to walk or run in the rain? Learners adjust walking and running speeds, rainfall intensity, and distance traveled to model water exposure and test real-world kinematics principles. The simulation visualizes relative motion between a person and falling raindrops to reveal optimal strategies for staying dry.

Learning objectives: Understand how velocity and direction affect relative motion between objects | Analyze the relationship between speed, distance, and precipitation exposure | Apply kinematic reasoning to optimize real-world problem-solving scenarios
