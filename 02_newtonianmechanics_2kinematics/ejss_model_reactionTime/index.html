About this Simulation

This simulator models the relationship between human reaction time and vehicle braking distance. Students observe how the delay between seeing a traffic light change and applying the brakes affects stopping distance, reinforcing key kinematics concepts through realistic, interactive scenarios.

Learning objectives: Understand how reaction time impacts total stopping distance | Explore the kinematic equations governing acceleration and deceleration | Analyse real-world factors affecting vehicle safety and motion
