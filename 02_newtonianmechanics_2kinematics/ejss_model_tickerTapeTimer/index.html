About this Simulation

This ticker tape kinematics simulation models the motion of objects using virtual ticker tape timing. Students observe how objects move, collect position data at regular intervals, and analyze velocity and displacement patterns. The interactive datatable and visual feedback help learners connect real-world motion to kinematic equations and graphs.

Learning objectives: Understand how ticker tape timers measure motion and calculate average velocity | Analyze motion graphs and interpret kinematic data from position measurements | Apply kinematics concepts to predict and explain object motion over time
