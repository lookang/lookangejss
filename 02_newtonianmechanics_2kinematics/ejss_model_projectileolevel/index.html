About this Simulation

This interactive projectile motion model allows learners to investigate how objects move under gravity when launched at different angles and speeds. By adjusting initial velocity, launch angle, and initial height, students observe real-time changes to trajectory paths, maximum height, and horizontal range, building intuitive understanding of two-dimensional motion.

Learning objectives: Understand how launch angle and initial velocity affect projectile trajectory and range | Analyse the independent horizontal and vertical components of motion | Predict and verify relationships between initial conditions and projectile outcomes
