About this Simulation

This simulation models a marble launched horizontally from a spring-loaded cart in motion. Learners observe how the marble's trajectory combines the cart's uniform velocity with the initial launch velocity, exploring relative reference frames and the independence of horizontal and vertical motion components.

Learning objectives: Understand how uniform speed affects projectile motion | Analyse velocity components in different reference frames | Predict and visualize paths of objects launched from moving platforms
