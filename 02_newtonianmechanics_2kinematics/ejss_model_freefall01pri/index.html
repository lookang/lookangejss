About this Simulation

This interactive model lets you explore one-dimensional free fall motion along the vertical (Y) axis. Adjust initial position and velocity, then observe real-time graphs showing how acceleration due to gravity influences displacement, velocity, and position. Experiment with different starting conditions to understand kinematic relationships.

Learning objectives: Understand how constant gravitational acceleration affects velocity and displacement in free fall | Interpret position-time and velocity-time graphs for vertical motion | Apply kinematic equations to predict motion under gravity
