About this Simulation

This interactive frog jumping simulation allows learners to explore kinematics by controlling jump parameters and observing the resulting trajectories. Students can adjust initial velocity, launch angle, and other variables to investigate how these factors affect the frog's flight path, landing distance, and time of flight.

Learning objectives: Understand how initial velocity and angle influence projectile motion | Analyze the relationship between horizontal and vertical motion components | Apply kinematic equations to predict and verify jumping distances
