About this Simulation

This interactive simulation demonstrates how Fourier transforms break down complex periodic waves into their constituent sine wave frequencies. Users adjust amplitude, frequency, and other parameters to synthesize custom waveforms while observing real-time sound output and spectral visualization. Perfect for understanding harmonic composition and wave superposition.

Learning objectives: Understand how periodic functions decompose into sine and cosine components | Explore the relationship between frequency domain and time domain representations | Discover how harmonic frequencies combine to create different waveform shapes
