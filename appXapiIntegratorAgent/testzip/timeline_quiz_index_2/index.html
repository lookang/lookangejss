How to Factor

  1. Look at the quadratic expression
  2. Drag x-terms to the top row and left column
  3. Drag constants to complete the factors
  4. The frame calculates products automatically
  5. Check if products sum to the original expression
  6. Click "Check Answer" to verify

Tip: For ax² + bx + c, find factors where the cross products sum to b!
