How to Play

Learning Objective: Understand different types of security challenges that arise from globalisation and how to categorize them.

Instructions:

  1. Click "Start Game" to begin
  2. Read each security challenge scenario carefully
  3. Drag and drop each challenge card into the correct category zone
  4. Categories include:
    • Cybersecurity: Digital threats and online security
    • Health Security: Disease spread and pandemics
    • Economic Security: Financial crimes and economic threats
    • Terrorism: Extremism and terrorist activities
  5. Click "Check Answers" when you've placed all cards
  6. Use "Hint" if you need help
  7. Progress through levels with increasing difficulty

Scoring:

  • ✅ Correct placement: +10 points
  • ❌ Incorrect placement: -5 points
  • Complete all challenges to advance to the next level
