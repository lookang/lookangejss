About this Simulation

This interactive quiz helps primary students discover and reflect on their 21st-century skills and future potential. Learners explore key competencies across different areas, receive personalized feedback, and gain insight into their strengths and development areas. The simulation encourages self-awareness and motivates students to consider their future pathways.

Learning objectives: Identify and assess personal 21st-century skills and competencies | Reflect on individual strengths and areas for growth | Explore connections between present abilities and future career possibilities
