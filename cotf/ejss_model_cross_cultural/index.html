About this Simulation

This simulation provides an interactive platform for exploring cross-cultural perspectives and building awareness of diverse worldviews. Learners engage with scenarios and variables that reflect cultural differences, enabling them to develop empathy, recognise bias, and understand how cultural contexts shape human behaviour and decision-making across global communities.

Learning objectives: Develop awareness of cultural diversity and recognise how perspectives vary across different cultures | Identify personal biases and explore strategies for building cross-cultural competence | Apply cultural understanding to real-world scenarios and global challenges
