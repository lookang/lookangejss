About this Simulation

The 21CC Navigator Future Self Quiz is an interactive tool that guides students through reflective questioning to envision their future selves. Learners explore their aspirations, values, and potential career pathways while considering the skills and decisions that shape their futures.

Learning objectives: Develop self-awareness and clarify personal aspirations for the future | Identify key skills and competencies needed to achieve long-term goals | Make informed decisions about learning pathways based on future self-reflection
