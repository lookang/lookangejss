About this Simulation

Setago is an interactive classroom simulation platform that models complex physics and mathematics phenomena through dynamic visual representations. Students can manipulate variables, observe real-time changes, and develop deeper understanding of scientific concepts through hands-on exploration and experimentation.

Learning objectives: Visualise abstract physics and mathematics principles through interactive models | Develop problem-solving skills by experimenting with simulation variables | Build future-ready competencies in STEM through active, inquiry-based learning
