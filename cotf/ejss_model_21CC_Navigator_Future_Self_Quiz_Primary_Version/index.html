About this Simulation

This interactive quiz guides primary students through a personalized exploration of their future selves and emerging 21st-century competencies. By answering thoughtful questions, learners discover their strengths, interests, and potential career pathways while developing self-awareness and critical thinking skills in an engaging, game-like environment.

Learning objectives: Reflect on personal strengths and future aspirations in relation to 21st-century skills | Explore diverse career pathways and their connection to individual interests and abilities | Develop self-awareness and metacognitive understanding through interactive self-assessment
