About this Simulation

This sustainability simulation enables learners to model real-world environmental systems and explore the interconnections between resource consumption, population dynamics, and ecological limits. Students can adjust variables and observe long-term effects on planetary systems, gaining insights into sustainable practices and environmental decision-making.

Learning objectives: Understand how resource depletion and consumption patterns affect environmental sustainability | Evaluate trade-offs between economic development and ecological preservation | Apply systems thinking to predict outcomes of sustainability policies
