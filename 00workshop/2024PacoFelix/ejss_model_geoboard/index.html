About this Simulation

This interactive geoboard template enables students to create and explore geometric shapes by placing pegs and stretching bands. Learners investigate properties of polygons, calculate area and perimeter, understand coordinates, and discover geometric relationships through hands-on experimentation in a digital environment.

Learning objectives: Construct and analyse geometric shapes using a virtual geoboard | Develop understanding of area, perimeter, and coordinate systems | Investigate geometric properties and spatial relationships through interactive exploration
