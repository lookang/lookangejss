About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models dynamic motion by allowing learners to manipulate key variables including position, velocity, and acceleration in real time. Students can adjust angle and initial conditions to observe how objects move and interact with physics principles, building intuitive understanding through direct experimentation.

Learning objectives: Understand relationships between position, velocity, and acceleration | Analyse how initial conditions affect motion trajectories | Apply mathematical and physical reasoning to predict and verify outcomes
