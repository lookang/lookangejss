About this Simulation

This interactive R0 infection model template allows learners to simulate how infectious diseases spread through populations. By adjusting transmission rates and other parameters, students explore the relationship between the basic reproduction number (R0) and epidemic outcomes, observing real-time changes in disease dynamics and population health impacts.

Learning objectives: Understand how R0 determines epidemic growth and disease persistence | Investigate the effects of transmission rate and recovery time on infection spread | Apply mathematical modeling to predict public health outcomes and intervention strategies
