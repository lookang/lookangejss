About this Simulation

This interactive simulation helps learners explore the relationships between words with identical or similar meanings in Tamil. Students practice matching synonyms across multiple rounds, strengthening their vocabulary and understanding of nuanced language use through engaging, self-paced interaction.

Learning objectives: Identify and match words with equivalent meanings | Develop deeper understanding of Tamil vocabulary and semantic relationships | Build confidence in recognizing synonyms through repeated interactive practice
