About this Simulation

RBSong is an interactive simulation that lets learners explore the relationship between musical notes, timing, and rhythm. Students can compose sequences, adjust note durations, control playback speed, and match their input to target songs. The simulation provides real-time feedback through scoring and tolerance settings, making it ideal for developing beat synchronization and musical timing skills.

Learning objectives: Understand note timing and rhythm accuracy in music | Practice beat synchronization and tempo control | Develop ear-training and performance feedback skills through interactive composition and scoring
