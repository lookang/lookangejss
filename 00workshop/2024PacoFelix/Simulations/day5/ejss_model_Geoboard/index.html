About this Simulation

This interactive geoboard simulation allows learners to create and explore geometric shapes by placing pegs and connecting them with bands. Students can investigate properties of polygons, calculate area and perimeter, and discover mathematical relationships through direct manipulation and visual feedback.

Learning objectives: Construct and identify various geometric shapes on a grid | Calculate and compare area and perimeter of different polygons | Develop spatial reasoning and geometric visualization skills
