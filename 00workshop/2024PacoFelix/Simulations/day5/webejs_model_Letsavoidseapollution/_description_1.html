Let's Avoid Sea Pollution!

The main goal of this game is to educate users about the importance of avoiding pollution in the ocean. By maneuvering the fish without touching the pollutants, users can learn about the challenges marine life faces due to pollution.

Game Elements

  1. Fish: The fish is positioned on the left side of the screen and can be dragged by the user. 
  2. Coral Reef: Located on the right side of the screen, it serves as the target destination for the fish.
  3. Pollutants: Several types of pollutants are present in the water, including:
    • a fish net
    • a plastic bottle 
    • a plastic bag 
  4. Turtle & octopus: These animals co-exist in the marine environment.

How to play the game

  1. A dialog box in the center of the screen provides instructions: "Drag the fish to reach the coral reef on the right without colliding into any floating pollutants." Click "Ok" button to start the game.
  2. Drag the fish to avoid collision with the pollutants and marine life in order to reach the coral reef located on the right of the screen.
