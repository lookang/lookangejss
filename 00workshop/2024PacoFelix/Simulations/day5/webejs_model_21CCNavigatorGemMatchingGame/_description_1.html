Card Matching Game

Introduction

As part of 21CC Navigator, students are expected to be aware of the Emerging 21st Century Competencies (E21CC). This application aims to make learning about E21CC more enjoyable and interesting for students.

What this game is

This is a simple memory-match game.

  • 18 cards (9 Gem cards) are faced down randomly on the play-space
  • Students click on two cards and if they match, they get a point.
  • If they don’t match, the student has to try again to pick a correct pair
  • They continue doing this until all 9 pairs are successfully matched
