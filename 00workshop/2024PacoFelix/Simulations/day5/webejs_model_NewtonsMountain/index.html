About this Simulation

NewtonsMountain models how objects launched from a mountain peak follow different trajectories depending on initial velocity. Learners explore the relationship between launch speed, gravitational force, and orbital motion—from falling back to Earth to achieving stable orbits and escape velocity in an interactive, visual environment.

Learning objectives: Understand how initial velocity affects orbital trajectories and gravitational escape conditions | Visualize the continuum between projectile motion, circular orbits, and escape trajectories | Apply Newton's laws to predict and explain satellite motion around a spherical body
