About this Simulation

This interactive fireworks simulation models the physics of projectile motion, explosions, and particle behavior. Learners can adjust launch angles, initial velocities, and explosion parameters to observe how forces affect trajectories and energy distribution. The simulation visualizes real-time particle interactions and gravitational effects.

Learning objectives: Understand how launch angle and velocity influence projectile motion | Explore energy conservation and transformation during explosions | Analyse vector forces and acceleration in multi-body particle systems
