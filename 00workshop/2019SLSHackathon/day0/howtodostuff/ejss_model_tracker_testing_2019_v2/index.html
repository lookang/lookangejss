About this Simulation

EjsS Tracker is an interactive physics simulation designed for analyzing and tracking motion in video footage. Learners can examine object trajectories, measure velocities and accelerations, and study real-world motion patterns. The tool supports detailed kinematic analysis by allowing frame-by-frame video examination and automated position tracking.

Learning objectives: Analyze motion from video data and extract kinematic variables | Develop skills in trajectory measurement and motion interpretation | Understand the relationship between position, velocity, and acceleration in real systems
