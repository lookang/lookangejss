Vertical Mass-Spring Oscillating System (with damping)

Tracker Experiment

Vertical mass-spring oscillating system with damping, using:
Total mass: 0.620 kg
6 rubber bands in series configuration
The vertical displacement - time graph follows an exponetial sinusoidal decay:
y = 0.121 exp (-0.294t) sin (5.31t + 4.59) + 0.123
