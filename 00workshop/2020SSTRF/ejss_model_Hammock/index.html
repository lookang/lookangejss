About this Simulation

This interactive hammock simulation models the physics of suspended objects under gravitational force. Students can adjust mass, gravity, and rubber band elasticity to observe how forces affect hammock displacement. Explore the relationships between weight, tension, and material properties through real-time visualization and interactive parameter control.

Learning objectives: Understand how gravitational force creates tension in suspended systems | Investigate the relationship between mass, gravity, and elastic deformation | Analyze equilibrium conditions in real-world structures
