About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models the physics of beach balls and tennis balls in flight, allowing learners to investigate how mass, spin rate, and air resistance influence trajectory and landing position. Students can adjust launch height, rotation speed, and viewing angles to explore real-world sports dynamics.

Learning objectives: Understand how angular velocity and spin affect projectile motion | Explore the relationship between ball mass, air resistance, and trajectory | Analyze the physics principles underlying realistic sports performance
