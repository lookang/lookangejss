About this Simulation

This simulation models the Magnus force—the sideways force acting on spinning objects moving through a fluid. Learners can adjust spin rate (omega), launch angle (theta), and observe how Magnus force curves ball trajectories in 3D space. Explore real-world applications in football, tennis, baseball and understand the physics behind curveballs and curved kicks.

Learning objectives: Understand how spin generates Magnus force and affects projectile motion | Visualize vector fields to see force direction and magnitude | Apply Magnus force concepts to explain sports phenomena and real-world scenarios
