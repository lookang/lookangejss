About this Simulation

This simulation allows learners to investigate how different materials respond to electric current. By testing various conductors and insulators, students explore the fundamental properties that determine whether materials allow or block electrical flow, building understanding of electrical conductivity and atomic structure.

Learning objectives: Distinguish between conductors and insulators based on electron movement | Identify everyday materials by their electrical properties | Explain how atomic structure relates to electrical conductivity
