About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models Rutherford's atomic model and demonstrates how alpha particles scatter when directed at atomic nuclei. Learners can adjust particle trajectories, observe nuclear interactions, and visualise the relationship between particle impact parameters and scattering angles to understand atomic structure fundamentals.

Learning objectives: Understand the structure of atoms according to Rutherford's model and the significance of the nucleus | Analyse how alpha particles scatter based on distance and energy parameters | Interpret experimental data linking particle deflection to nuclear charge and mass distribution
