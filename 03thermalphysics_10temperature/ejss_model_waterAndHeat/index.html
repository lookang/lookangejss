About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows students to compare heat energy across different volumes and temperatures of water. By adjusting water volume and temperature values, learners investigate the relationship between these variables and total thermal energy, exploring how both mass and temperature contribute to heat content.

Learning objectives: Understand that heat energy depends on both mass (volume) and temperature | Compare thermal energy in water samples with different volumes and temperatures | Develop understanding of the distinction between temperature and heat energy
