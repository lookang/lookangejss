Interactive Condensation Lab: How does the temperature of a surface affect the rate of condensation?
 
In this 2D simulation, you will explore how the temperature of a surface affects the rate of condensation. You can observe how a tilted steel plate causes warm water vapour to lose heat and condense into tiny water droplets at different speeds based on how cold the plate is.

 

🧪 Instructions for Students
  

Follow these steps to investigate the relationship between surface temperature and condensation:
Step 1: Observe the Setup The water in the beaker is already boiling, creating a steady stream of warm water vapour that rises toward the tilted steel plate.

Step 2:  Set the steel plate to 30OC. Watch the underside of the plate to see how long it takes for droplets to form and drip into the dish.

Step 3: Now, set the plate temperature to less than 30OC. Notice if the water droplets form faster or more frequently compared to the room temperature setting. Next, try at a higher temperature.

Step 4: Draw a Conclusion Observe the collection dish. Does a colder surface lead to a higher rate of condensation?
