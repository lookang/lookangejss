About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models the process of evaporation and allows learners to investigate how multiple factors influence evaporation rates. Students can independently manipulate surface area, temperature, humidity levels, wind presence, and atmospheric pressure to observe real-time changes in evaporation behaviour and develop deeper understanding of molecular kinetics and phase changes.

Learning objectives: Identify and explain how temperature, surface area, and wind speed affect evaporation rates | Predict evaporation outcomes when multiple variables change simultaneously | Understand the relationship between atmospheric pressure, humidity, and phase change processes
