About this Simulation

This Spectrum Analyzer app enables learners to visualize and measure light intensity across different wavelengths of the electromagnetic spectrum. Using dual-camera inputs and real-time graphing tools, students can explore how light behaves, analyze spectral data, and understand the relationship between wavelength and intensity in authentic experimental contexts.

Learning objectives: Understand the properties and characteristics of the electromagnetic spectrum | Analyze light intensity measurements and interpret spectral graphs | Apply camera-based data collection to model real-world optical phenomena
