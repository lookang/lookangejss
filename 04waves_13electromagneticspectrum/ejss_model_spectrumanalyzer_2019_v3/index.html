About this Simulation

This spectrum analyzer simulation enables learners to visualize and analyze light intensity across different wavelengths of the electromagnetic spectrum. Students can explore spectral data through interactive graphing, examine intensity distributions, and understand how different light sources produce unique spectral signatures in real time.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between wavelength and light intensity in the electromagnetic spectrum | Interpret spectral data and identify characteristics of different light sources | Apply visualization tools to analyze and compare spectral patterns
