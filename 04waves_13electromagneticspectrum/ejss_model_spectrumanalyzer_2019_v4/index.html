About this Simulation

This spectrum analyzer app allows learners to visualize and analyze light intensity across different wavelengths of the electromagnetic spectrum. Using camera input or preset data, students can explore spectral patterns, identify characteristic wavelengths, and understand how light intensity varies across the spectrum in real-world scenarios.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between wavelength and light intensity | Analyze spectral data and interpret electromagnetic spectrum distributions | Apply camera-based measurements to real-world light analysis
