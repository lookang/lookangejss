Animal Classification Help

Mammals: Dog, Cat, Elephant - They have fur/hair and feed milk to babies
Birds: Eagle, Penguin, Parrot - They have feathers and wings
Reptiles: Snake, Lizard, Turtle - They have scales and are cold-blooded
Amphibians: Frog, Toad, Salamander - They live both in water and on land
Fish: Goldfish, Shark, Tuna - They live in water and breathe through gills
Insects: Butterfly, Ant, Bee - They have 6 legs and 3 body parts
