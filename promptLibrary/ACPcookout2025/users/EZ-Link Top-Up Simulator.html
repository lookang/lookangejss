How to Use the EZ-Link Top-Up Simulator

  1. Start: Touch the screen to begin
  2. Choose Payment: Select Cash, NETS, or Credit Card
  3. Select Amount: Choose $5, $10, $20, or $50
  4. Pay: Drag money/cards from wallet or use keypad for PIN
  5. Place EZ-Link: Drag your EZ-Link card to the reader
  6. Complete: Collect receipt and check new balance

Tips: You can drag items from your wallet to the appropriate slots. Practice as many times as you want!
