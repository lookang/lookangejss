🏛️ Budget Blueprint SG

You are Singapore's Finance Minister.

Over 4 Fiscal Years, allocate limited government resources wisely to keep Singapore thriving. Every decision involves a trade-off!

1
Deploy your 3 Teams 🏛️🏛️🏛️ — each is a generic team you send to any sector (MOH, MTI…) to earn that sector's Funds or Manpower. Teams reset each year.
2
Fund National Priorities — spend your resources on projects that earn NWP (National Well-being Points)
3
All 3 Ministry Teams must be deployed before you can end the fiscal year
4
Each year brings a Global Event — watch the yellow banner and adapt your plan!
