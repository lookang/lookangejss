How to Use the Simulator

  • Angle of Elevation: The angle above the horizontal when looking up at an object
  • Angle of Depression: The angle below the horizontal when looking down at an object
  • Move the observer: Use the slider or arrow buttons to change position
  • Change objects: Select different objects to observe (building, tree, lighthouse)
  • Switch views: Toggle between ground view and helicopter view
  • Observe: Watch how the angle changes as you move closer or farther from the object
