How to Play Clue Chase

Objective: Catch the correct vocabulary cards that match the given prompt!

Controls:

  • Use arrow keys ⬅️➡️ or touch buttons to move the detective
  • Catch falling cards by positioning the detective underneath
  • Match words to definitions or definitions to words

Scoring:

  • Correct catch: +10 points
  • Streak bonus: +5 extra points per consecutive correct answer
  • Wrong catch or miss: -1 life

Power-ups:

  • 5 streak: Slow motion for 5 seconds
  • 10 streak: Double points for 10 seconds
