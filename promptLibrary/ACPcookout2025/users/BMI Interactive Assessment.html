BMI helps us understand if our body weight is healthy for our height

Explore:
  • What happens to BMI when you increase mass but keep height the same?
    BMI increases linearly — double the mass means double the BMI. Mass and BMI are directly proportional.
  • How does height affect BMI?
    Increasing height lowers BMI — and the effect is squared, so even small height increases make a big difference.
  • Try to find a combination that gives a healthy BMI
    Example: 65 kg and 1.70 m → BMI = 65 ÷ (1.70 × 1.70) = 22.5 ✅
