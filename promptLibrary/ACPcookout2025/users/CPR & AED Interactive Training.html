🔍 🔍 Chain of Survival Analysis

The Chain of Survival has 4 critical links. Any missing link decreases survival chances!

  1. Early Recognition & Call for Help (Call 995)
  2. Early CPR (Chest compressions + rescue breathing)
  3. Early Defibrillation (AED use)
  4. Early Advanced Care (Paramedics & hospital)
