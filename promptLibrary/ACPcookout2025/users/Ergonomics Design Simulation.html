Ergonomic Design Tips

Chair Setup:

  • Feet should rest flat on floor or footrest
  • Thighs parallel to floor (90-110° knee angle)
  • Back angle between 100-110° for comfort
  • Elbow angle 90-120° when typing

General Principles:

  • Avoid excessive reach angles beyond 25°
  • Design for 5th to 95th percentile coverage
  • Consider adjustability for user diversity
