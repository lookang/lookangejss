Balanced vs Unbalanced Plates

✓ Balanced Plate

• ¼ plate grains (rice/noodles)

• ¼ plate protein (fish/tofu)

• ½ plate vegetables and fruits

✗ Unbalanced Plate

• Too much protein

• Not enough vegetables

• Missing fruits
