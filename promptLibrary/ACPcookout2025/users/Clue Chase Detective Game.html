How to Play Clue Chase

Objective: Catch falling vocabulary cards that match the given prompt!

Controls:

  • Use arrow keys or touch buttons to move the detective
  • Catch the correct vocabulary card to earn points
  • Avoid wrong answers - they cost you a life!

Game Modes:

  • Definition → Word: Match definitions to words
  • Word → Definition: Match words to definitions

Power-ups:

  • Slow Time: Cards fall slower (5 correct in a row)
  • Double Points: Earn 2x points (10 correct in a row)
