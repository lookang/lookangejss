Create a single-file HTML5 interactive that visually demonstrates a rearrangement proof of Pythagoras’ Theorem for Primary 5–6 students.
GOAL OF THE INTERACTIVE
The interactive must show that the area of the square on the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the areas of the two squares on the other sides:
a² + b² = c²
The proof should work like this:
- A right-angled triangle with side lengths a, b, and c is used.
- Four congruent copies of the triangle are arranged inside a large square.
- A slider rotates two of the triangles smoothly from one arrangement to another.
- At one end of the slider (0°), students should see the arrangement forming a central square labelled c².
- At the other end of the slider (90°), students should see the same total area rearranged into two separate squares labelled a² and b².
- The motion should make it visually clear that the triangles are only being rearranged, so the uncovered area changes form but keeps the same total area.
- The interactive should help students conclude that c² = a² + b².
IMPORTANT DESIGN REFERENCE
Model the interaction based on a rotating rearrangement proof:
- A pink triangle stays fixed at the top-left area.
- A yellow triangle stays fixed at the bottom-left area.
- A blue triangle and a green triangle rotate as the slider moves.
- At 0°, the interior uncovered region is a square labelled c².
- At 90°, the uncovered regions become two squares labelled a² and b².
- The transition between these states must be smooth and continuous.
