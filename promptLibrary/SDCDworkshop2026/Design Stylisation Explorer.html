Understanding Design Stylisation

  • Realistic: Accurate representation with detail and depth
  • Cartoon: Simplified forms with bold outlines and flat colors
  • Abstract: Non-representational, focusing on shapes and colors
  • Impressionist: Capturing light and mood with loose brushwork

Key Concepts: Stylisation involves simplifying, exaggerating, or transforming visual elements to create a specific aesthetic or communicate ideas more effectively.
