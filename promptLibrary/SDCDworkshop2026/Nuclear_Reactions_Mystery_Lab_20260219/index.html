🔬 What You Learned:

💥 Nuclear Fission:

A heavy nucleus breaks apart into lighter nuclei, releasing energy. Common in nuclear power plants and atomic weapons.

Example: Uranium-235 → Barium-141 + Krypton-92 + neutrons

✨ Nuclear Fusion:

Light nuclei join together to form heavier nuclei, releasing tremendous energy. Powers the Sun and stars.

Example: Deuterium + Tritium → Helium-4 + neutron
