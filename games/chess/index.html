Interactive Chess Simulation

How to Play

This interactive chess simulation lets you play either against another human or the computer. Click on a piece to see its possible moves (highlighted in red). When a move is made, a blue arrow shows the move direction. The game ends when a king is captured. In "Against Computer" mode, you can choose the difficulty: Novice (random moves), Intermediate (prefers capturing moves), or Expert (chooses the highest–value capture). Use the Reset button to restart.
