About this Simulation

This master template simulation allows learners to construct and visualize custom energy diagrams across multiple physics scenarios. Students can explore energy transformations, modify variables, and generate their own LOL energy diagram examples to deepen understanding of energy concepts and system behavior.

Learning objectives: Construct and interpret energy diagrams for diverse physical systems | Analyze energy transformations and conservation principles through interactive exploration | Apply template-based modeling to generate and test custom physics scenarios
