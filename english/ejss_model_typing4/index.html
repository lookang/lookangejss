About this Simulation

Bubble Typer is an interactive typing game where students practice keyboarding skills by typing words to pop bubbles before they reach the top. Learners explore the relationship between typing speed, accuracy, and successful gameplay while developing muscle memory and improving their overall typing proficiency through engaging, game-based practice.

Learning objectives: Develop touch typing skills and increase typing speed and accuracy | Practice proper keystroke techniques in an interactive, game-based environment | Build confidence in keyboard navigation and word recognition
