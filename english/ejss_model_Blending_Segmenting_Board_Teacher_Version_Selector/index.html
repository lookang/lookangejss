About this Simulation

This interactive blending and segmenting board enables teachers to guide students through phonetic sound manipulation in real time. Learners explore how individual phonemes combine to form words (blending) and how words break into component sounds (segmenting), building foundational decoding and encoding skills essential for early reading development.

Learning objectives: Develop phonemic awareness by blending individual sounds into recognizable words | Practice segmenting words into constituent phonemes to strengthen spelling and sound recognition | Build fluency with sound-symbol relationships through teacher-guided interactive exploration
