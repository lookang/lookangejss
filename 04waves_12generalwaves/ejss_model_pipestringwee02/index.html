About this Simulation

This simulation models standing waves formed inside pipes with different boundary conditions. Learners can adjust frequency, amplitude, and end conditions (open or closed) to observe how resonance patterns emerge. Explore the relationship between pipe length, wavelength, and the fundamental and harmonic frequencies that produce standing waves.

Learning objectives: Understand how standing waves form in pipes and the role of boundary conditions | Predict resonant frequencies based on pipe length and wavelength | Visualize the difference between open and closed pipe resonance patterns
