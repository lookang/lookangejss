About this Simulation

The Wave Machine simulation allows learners to investigate fundamental wave properties by controlling parameters such as wavelength, amplitude, frequency, and driver motion. Students can observe how waves propagate, reflect, and interact in real time, building intuition about wave behavior through hands-on exploration and experimentation.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between wavelength, frequency, and wave speed | Visualize how amplitude and driver settings affect wave propagation | Explore wave phenomena including reflection and superposition in dynamic systems
