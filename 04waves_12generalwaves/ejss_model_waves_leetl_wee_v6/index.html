About this Simulation

This simulation models transverse wave motion, allowing learners to visualize how particles oscillate perpendicular to wave direction. Students can adjust amplitude, wavelength, and frequency to observe real-time changes in wave behavior, exploring the relationship between these fundamental wave properties and their physical effects.

Learning objectives: Understand the definition and characteristics of transverse waves | Investigate the relationship between amplitude, wavelength, frequency, and wave speed | Apply visual observation to predict how wave properties affect oscillation patterns
