About this Simulation

This simulation models standing wave formation in pipes with open and closed ends. Students visualize how waves reflect at boundaries, creating interference patterns. By adjusting frequency, wavelength, and amplitude, learners explore resonance conditions and discover relationships between pipe length, wave speed, and harmonic frequencies.

Learning objectives: Understand how boundary conditions determine standing wave patterns | Calculate wavelength and frequency relationships in resonating pipes | Predict harmonic frequencies for open and closed pipe configurations
