About this Simulation

The Wave Space Time Simulator lets students explore fundamental wave properties by visualizing how waves evolve and propagate through space and time. Adjust wavelength, frequency, amplitude, and observation parameters to observe real-time changes in wave behavior and develop intuition about wave dynamics.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between wavelength, frequency, and wave speed | Visualize wave propagation and spacetime representation of wave motion | Investigate how changing parameters affects wave characteristics and behavior
