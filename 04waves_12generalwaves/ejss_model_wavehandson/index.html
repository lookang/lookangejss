About this Simulation

Wave Handson is an interactive physics simulation that lets learners visualize and manipulate fundamental wave properties in real time. Students can adjust amplitude, frequency, wavelength, and other parameters to observe how waves behave, interact, and change under different conditions.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between frequency, wavelength, and wave speed | Visualize how changing amplitude and frequency affects wave motion | Explore wave phenomena including interference and superposition through hands-on experimentation
