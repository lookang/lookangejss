About this Simulation

This simulation models the propagation and behavior of wave impulses across a medium. Learners can adjust parameters such as wave size, stroke width, damping, and pulse frequency to observe how disturbances travel and interact with boundaries. Real-time visualization helps students develop intuitive understanding of wave motion and energy transfer.

Learning objectives: Observe how wave impulses propagate through a medium | Investigate the effects of amplitude, wavelength, and damping on wave behavior | Understand energy transfer and wave interactions in physical systems
