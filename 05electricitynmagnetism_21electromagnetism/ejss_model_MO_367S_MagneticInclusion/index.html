About this Simulation

This simulation models the behavior of charged particles within magnetic field regions, allowing learners to visualize real-time motion dynamics. By adjusting particle properties—mass, charge, velocity, and position—students observe how electromagnetic forces govern trajectory, acceleration, and confinement patterns in magnetic inclusion systems.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between charge, velocity, and magnetic force in particle motion | Analyze how mass and charge affect particle trajectories in magnetic fields | Apply vector concepts to predict and explain electromagnetic particle behavior
