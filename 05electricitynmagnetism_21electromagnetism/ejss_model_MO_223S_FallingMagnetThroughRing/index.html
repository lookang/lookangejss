About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models a permanent magnet falling through a conducting ring under gravity. As the magnet descends, it induces currents in the ring that create opposing magnetic forces, demonstrating electromagnetic induction and magnetic damping. Learners can adjust gravity, observe position changes, and explore how magnetic fields interact with moving conductors.

Learning objectives: Understand how changing magnetic flux induces currents in conductors | Explore Lenz's law through real-time observation of magnetic braking effects | Investigate the relationship between magnet velocity, induced currents, and electromagnetic forces
