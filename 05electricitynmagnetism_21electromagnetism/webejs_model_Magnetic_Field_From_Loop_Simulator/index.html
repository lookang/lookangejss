About this Simulation

This interactive simulator visualizes the magnetic field produced by a current-carrying loop in three dimensions. Learners can adjust loop parameters, observe how field strength and direction vary in space, and explore the relationship between current geometry and magnetic field patterns in real time.

Learning objectives: Understand how current loops generate magnetic fields | Visualize field vectors and patterns in 3D space | Investigate how loop size and position affect magnetic field distribution
