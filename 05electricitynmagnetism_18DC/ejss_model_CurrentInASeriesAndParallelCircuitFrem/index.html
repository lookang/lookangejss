About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models current behavior in series and parallel circuit configurations. Learners can visualize how electric current flows through different circuit paths, observe current values at various points, and explore how circuit topology affects current distribution and circuit behavior.

Learning objectives: Understand how current behaves differently in series versus parallel circuits | Predict and measure current at multiple points in complex circuits | Apply Kirchhoff's current law to analyze current distribution in circuit networks
