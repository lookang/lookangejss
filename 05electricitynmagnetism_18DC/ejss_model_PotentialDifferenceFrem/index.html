About this Simulation

This simulation models potential difference in DC circuits, allowing learners to visualise how voltage distributes across circuit components. By manipulating circuit variables and observing real-time changes, students develop intuitive understanding of electrical potential, current flow, and energy transfer in practical circuits.

Learning objectives: Understand how potential difference drives current through circuits | Analyse voltage distribution across series and parallel components | Predict circuit behaviour by applying relationships between voltage, current, and resistance
