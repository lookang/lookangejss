About this Simulation

This simulation models a variable resistor in a DC circuit, allowing learners to adjust resistance dynamically and observe real-time changes in current and voltage. Students can manipulate resistance values, explore circuit behaviour, and visualise how resistor position and width affect electrical properties.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between resistance, current, and voltage in DC circuits | Investigate how variable resistors control current flow | Analyse circuit behaviour through interactive measurement and observation
