About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows learners to investigate how ammeters function in DC circuits. Students can experiment with ammeter placement, observe current measurements across different circuit configurations, and explore the relationship between power supply, circuit components, and current flow in real-time.

Learning objectives: Understand how ammeters measure electric current in series circuits | Apply correct ammeter connection techniques and interpret current readings | Investigate how circuit variables affect current flow and ammeter measurements
