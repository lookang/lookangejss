About this Simulation

This simulation allows learners to design and test circuits that power an alarm clock. Students experiment with different component arrangements, manipulate circuit parameters, and observe how electrical circuits function in real-world applications. Through interactive exploration, users develop understanding of circuit behavior and troubleshooting.

Learning objectives: Understand how DC circuits are constructed and how components work together to deliver power | Analyse the relationship between circuit configuration and device functionality | Apply circuit design principles to solve practical problems
