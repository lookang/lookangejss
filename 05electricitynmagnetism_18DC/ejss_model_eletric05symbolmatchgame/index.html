About this Simulation

This interactive symbol matching game challenges learners to classify household and laboratory appliances as either AC (alternating current) or DC (direct current) powered devices. Students match appliance images to their correct power type while receiving real-time feedback, reinforcing understanding of circuit fundamentals and practical electrical applications.

Learning objectives: Distinguish between AC and DC power sources and their applications | Identify common appliances and their electrical requirements | Develop fluency in recognizing circuit symbols and electrical device classifications
