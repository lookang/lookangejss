About this Simulation

Circuitry At Home models real-world DC circuits where learners design and manipulate circuits using power sources, bulbs, and wiring. Students can adjust circuit configurations, control power levels, and modify bulb sizes to observe how these variables affect brightness and current distribution across series and parallel arrangements.

Learning objectives: Understand the differences between series and parallel circuits | Predict and observe how voltage, resistance, and brightness change with circuit configuration | Apply circuit principles to practical household electrical systems
