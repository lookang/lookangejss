About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows students to build and analyse different types of DC circuits. By manipulating switches and adjusting bulb properties, learners can observe how circuit configuration affects current flow, brightness, and overall circuit behaviour in real time.

Learning objectives: Distinguish between series and parallel circuits and their characteristics | Predict how adding or removing switches affects bulb brightness and current distribution | Explain the relationship between circuit type and component performance
