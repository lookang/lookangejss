About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models how matter responds to heating, allowing learners to manipulate heat input and observe corresponding temperature changes and thermal behaviour. Students can explore the relationship between heat energy and temperature across different modes, adjusting variables to investigate thermal properties and energy transfer in real-time.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between heat and temperature change in matter | Investigate how thermal properties affect heating and cooling rates | Apply experimental variables to model real-world thermal phenomena
