About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models the cooling of a 0.1 kg metal block immersed in 100ml of water. Learners observe real-time temperature changes, plot cooling curves, and explore how thermal energy transfers between objects. Adjust variables and analyse the relationship between time, mass, and cooling rates.

Learning objectives: Understand the principles of heat transfer and thermal equilibrium | Analyse cooling curves and interpret temperature-time graphs | Investigate how mass and thermal properties affect cooling rates
