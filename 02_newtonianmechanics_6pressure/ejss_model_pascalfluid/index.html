About this Simulation

This simulation models how pressure transmits through fluids connecting two objects, demonstrating Pascal's principle in action. Learners can adjust object properties (mass and spring constants) and observe real-time pressure changes, exploring how forces distribute across interconnected fluid systems and how equilibrium is maintained.

Learning objectives: Understand how pressure transmits equally through connected fluids | Investigate relationships between applied force, area, and resulting pressure | Predict and verify pressure equilibrium in multi-object fluid systems
