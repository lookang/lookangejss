## Description
Learn Archimedes principle using a real interactive simulation.

In this lesson, we explore how density affects floating, sinking, and neutral buoyancy. We also look at how the weight of displaced liquid relates to upthrust, and how the spring scale and lower scale change during the motion.

This tutorial covers:
- density of the solution
- cube mass and cube volume
- show weight
- show upthrust
- Teacher mode
- Equation mode
- solution density 0.6 g/cm^3
- solution density 1.0 g/cm^3
- equal density and neutral buoyancy
- solution density 2.0 g/cm^3
