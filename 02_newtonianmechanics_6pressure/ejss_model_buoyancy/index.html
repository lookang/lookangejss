About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models buoyancy and pressure by allowing learners to observe how objects behave in fluids. Students can adjust object mass, position, and fluid properties to explore the relationship between buoyant force, weight, and fluid displacement in real-time.

Learning objectives: Understand how buoyant force depends on fluid density and displaced volume | Investigate the conditions for floating, sinking, and neutral buoyancy | Apply pressure and force concepts to explain real-world buoyancy phenomena
