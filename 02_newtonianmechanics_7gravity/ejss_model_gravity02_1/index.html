About this Simulation

This interactive binary stars model enables learners to visualize how two massive objects orbit their common center of mass under mutual gravitational attraction. Adjust orbital parameters, mass ratios, and viewing angles to observe how gravity governs stellar motion, orbital shapes, and system dynamics in real-time.

Learning objectives: Understand how gravitational force maintains binary star orbits | Explore the relationship between mass, distance, and orbital period | Analyze center of mass concepts and orbital mechanics principles
