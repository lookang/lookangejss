Periodic orbits in the N-body plane problem

N equal masses are moving in the same plane under the action of mutual gravitational attraction. If the initial conditions are chosen in the right way, some periodic solutions of a very special kind may be found: all particles move along the same orbit and the total angular momentum vanishes in the center-of-mass system. One can see some examples in this simulation.
