About this Simulation

This simulation models periodic and chaotic orbits under gravitational influence. Learners can adjust orbital parameters, observe how initial conditions affect trajectory shapes, and explore the boundary between stable periodic motion and chaotic behavior in gravitational systems.

Learning objectives: Understand how orbital shape depends on initial velocity and position | Distinguish between periodic, elliptical, and chaotic orbital patterns | Visualize the relationship between energy, angular momentum, and orbital stability
