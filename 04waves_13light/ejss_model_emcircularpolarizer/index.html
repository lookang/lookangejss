About this Simulation

This simulation models how circular polarizers manipulate electromagnetic waves and light. Learners can adjust multiple parameters to observe the transformation of linearly polarized light into circularly polarized light, visualizing electric field components and their phase relationships in real time.

Learning objectives: Understand the principles of circular polarization and how polarizers work | Analyse the relationship between electric field components (Ex and Ez) in producing circular polarization | Explore how adjusting polarizer properties affects wave propagation and polarization state
