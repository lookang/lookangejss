About this Simulation

This simulation models how mirrors reflect light in three-dimensional space viewed through a virtual window. Learners can manipulate mirror orientation, position, and size while observing how light rays reflect in real-time. By adjusting viewing angles and mirror parameters, students develop intuitive understanding of geometric optics and reflection laws in 3D environments.

Learning objectives: Understand how angle of incidence equals angle of reflection in three dimensions | Visualise the relationship between mirror orientation and reflected light paths | Apply geometric principles to predict and verify reflection behaviour in complex spatial configurations
