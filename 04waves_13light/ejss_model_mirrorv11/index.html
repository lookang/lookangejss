About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models vertical mirror reflection, allowing learners to explore how light reflects off vertical mirrors using point objects, customisable shapes (Wink, Bear, Large Object), and user-written text. Students can manipulate object positions and observe real-time reflection patterns to understand the law of reflection and mirror geometry principles.

Learning objectives: Understand how vertical mirrors reflect light and objects according to the law of reflection | Explore the relationship between object position and image formation in plane mirrors | Apply reflection principles by predicting and verifying image positions for various objects
