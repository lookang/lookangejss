About this Simulation

This prism light trace simulator models how light rays bend as they enter and exit prisms at various angles. Learners can adjust incident angles, prism size, and orientation to observe real-time ray paths and trace refraction patterns, exploring Snell's law and optical behavior dynamically.

Learning objectives: Understand how light refracts at prism boundaries following Snell's law | Predict and observe light ray paths through different prism orientations and angles | Investigate the relationship between incident angle and refraction angle in optical systems
