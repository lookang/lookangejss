About this Simulation

This interactive 3D simulation allows learners to explore boxes and prisms by manipulating their dimensions and observing real-time changes in volume and surface area. Students can rotate, scale, and investigate how dimension changes affect geometric properties, building intuitive understanding of three-dimensional shapes.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between dimensions and volume in rectangular prisms | Calculate and visualize surface area and volume of 3D boxes | Develop spatial reasoning skills through interactive 3D exploration
