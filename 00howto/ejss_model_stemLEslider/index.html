About this Simulation

This simulation enables learners to explore key design considerations in STEM education by manipulating interactive sliders to adjust variables affecting learning experiences. Students investigate how different pedagogical approaches, resource allocation, and engagement strategies influence educational outcomes and student performance across science and mathematics contexts.

Learning objectives: Analyse how design variables impact STEM learning effectiveness | Evaluate trade-offs between different educational design choices | Apply systems thinking to optimise interactive science learning experiences
