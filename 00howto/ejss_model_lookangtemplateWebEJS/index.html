About this Simulation

WebEJS is a powerful open-source platform for creating interactive physics and mathematics simulations. Students can explore complex concepts through dynamic visualizations, manipulate variables in real-time, and observe immediate results. Teachers benefit from customizable templates and easy deployment in classroom and remote learning environments.

Learning objectives: Understand scientific principles through interactive experimentation | Develop problem-solving skills by manipulating simulation parameters | Connect abstract concepts to observable physical phenomena
