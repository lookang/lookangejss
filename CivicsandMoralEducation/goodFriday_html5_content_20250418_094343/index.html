The Events of Good Friday

The Last Supper

Jesus shared a final meal with his disciples, establishing the tradition of communion.

Garden of Gethsemane

Jesus prayed in the garden before his arrest, showing his human struggle and divine obedience.

The Trial

Jesus faced multiple trials before religious and Roman authorities.

The Crucifixion

Jesus was crucified at Golgotha, fulfilling prophecies and offering salvation.
