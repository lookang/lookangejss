About this Simulation

The Foutan Board is an interactive electricity simulator that models practical circuits with multiple switches and counters. Students can manipulate switch positions, observe real-time circuit behavior, and explore how different switch configurations affect circuit logic and operation.

Learning objectives: Understand how switches control electrical circuits | Investigate circuit logic through interactive switch combinations | Apply practical circuit design principles in a safe virtual environment
