Formula Used:

BAC (%) = (0.0116 × X) / (R × M) × 100 - 0.015 × t

  • X: Number of glasses of beer
  • M: Mass of person (kg)
  • R: Body water ratio (0.55 for female, 0.60 for male)
  • t: Time after drinking (hours)

BAC Effects:

  • 0.00%: Sober
  • 0.02%: Slight mood alteration, relaxation
  • 0.05%: Lowered alertness, impaired judgment
  • 0.08%: Legal limit in Singapore - Reduced coordination, impaired judgment
  • 0.10%: Slurred speech, slowed thinking
  • 0.15%: Loss of balance, muscle control
  • 0.30%+: Alcohol poisoning risk
  • 0.40%+: Potentially fatal
