About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows learners to visualize the superposition of two waves in real-time. Students can adjust wave properties such as amplitude, frequency, and phase difference to observe how waves combine, interact, and produce interference patterns. The simulation dynamically displays both individual waves and their resultant superposition.

Learning objectives: Understand the principle of wave superposition and how waves add together | Identify and observe constructive and destructive interference patterns | Explore the relationship between phase difference and interference effects
