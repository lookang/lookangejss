About this Simulation

This master template enables learners to construct and explore personalized energy diagrams across diverse real-world scenarios. Students visualize energy transformations, analyze work-energy relationships, and investigate power calculations dynamically. The customizable framework supports experimentation with different systems, making abstract energy concepts tangible and memorable through hands-on discovery.

Learning objectives: Create and interpret energy diagrams to model energy transfer in physical systems | Analyze relationships between work, kinetic energy, and potential energy through interactive exploration | Design custom scenarios to investigate how power varies with energy changes and time intervals
