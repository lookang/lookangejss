About this Simulation

This master template empowers learners to construct and customise LOL energy diagrams for unlimited scenarios. Students explore energy transformations, work-energy relationships, and power calculations by designing their own systems. The interactive builder reinforces how energy flows, converts between forms, and relates to real-world applications.

Learning objectives: Apply energy conservation principles to model diverse physical scenarios | Analyse relationships between work, energy transfer, and power in custom systems | Design and interpret energy diagrams to solve quantitative and qualitative problems
