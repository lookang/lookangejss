About this Simulation

This simulation models how solar panels generate electricity throughout a single day in Singapore, located 1° north of the equator. Learners observe how the sun's changing position from sunrise to sunset affects solar panel output, exploring the relationship between solar angle, radiation intensity, and power generation in a tropical climate.

Learning objectives: Understand how solar panel efficiency varies with sun angle throughout the day | Calculate and compare energy output across different times of day | Apply energy and power concepts to real-world renewable energy systems
