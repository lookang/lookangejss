About this Simulation

This master template enables learners to construct and visualize custom energy diagrams for any scenario. Students explore how energy transforms between different forms, observe work-energy relationships, and analyze power in real-world contexts. The flexible framework supports unlimited LOL (Levels of Learning) scenarios, fostering deeper understanding of energy conservation principles.

Learning objectives: Construct accurate energy diagrams to represent energy transfers in physical systems | Quantify relationships between work, energy changes, and power in various scenarios | Apply energy conservation principles to predict and explain outcomes in custom-designed situations
