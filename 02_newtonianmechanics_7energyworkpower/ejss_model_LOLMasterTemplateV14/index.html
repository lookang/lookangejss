About this Simulation

This master template empowers learners to generate unlimited customized energy diagrams across diverse scenarios. Students explore energy transformations, work-energy relationships, and power calculations by building their own LOL (Levels of Learning) diagrams, reinforcing deep understanding of energy concepts through hands-on experimentation and discovery.

Learning objectives: Visualize and model energy transformations in real-world scenarios | Analyze relationships between work, energy, and power through interactive diagram construction | Apply energy conservation principles to create and solve personalized physics problems
