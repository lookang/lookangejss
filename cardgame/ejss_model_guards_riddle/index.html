About this Simulation

The Guard's Riddle simulation is an interactive puzzle game that challenges learners to position numbered guards at distances equal to their numerical values. Students explore spatial reasoning, number relationships, and logical problem-solving by manipulating guard positions and observing constraint-based outcomes.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between numbers and physical distance | Apply logical reasoning to solve constraint-based puzzles | Develop spatial visualization and mathematical thinking skills
