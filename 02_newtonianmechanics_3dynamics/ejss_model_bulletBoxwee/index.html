About this Simulation

This simulator models the collision between a bullet and a box, allowing learners to observe and measure momentum transfer, energy changes, and motion outcomes. Adjust bullet velocity, mass, and box properties to explore how collision parameters affect the system's behaviour in real-time.

Learning objectives: Understand momentum conservation in collision systems | Investigate energy loss during inelastic collisions | Predict and analyse motion outcomes based on initial conditions
