About this Simulation

This simulation models one-dimensional collisions between objects, allowing learners to explore how momentum is conserved during interactions. Students can adjust initial velocities and masses, observe collision outcomes, and verify momentum conservation principles in real-time with dynamic visualizations.

Learning objectives: Understand momentum conservation in one-dimensional collisions | Distinguish between elastic and inelastic collision types | Predict post-collision velocities using momentum principles
