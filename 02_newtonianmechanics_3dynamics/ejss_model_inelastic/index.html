About this Simulation

This simulation models inelastic collisions between two objects, allowing learners to visualize how momentum is conserved while kinetic energy is lost during impact. By adjusting initial velocities, masses, and collision parameters, students can explore the relationship between collision type, energy dissipation, and post-collision motion in real-time.

Learning objectives: Understand conservation of momentum in inelastic collisions | Investigate the difference between elastic and inelastic collisions through energy analysis | Predict and verify post-collision velocities using momentum principles
