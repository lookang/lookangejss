About this Simulation

This simulation models Newton's laws of motion in action, allowing learners to explore how objects move and interact under various forces. Students can manipulate friction, velocity, and applied forces to observe real-time changes in motion, acceleration, and equilibrium. Perfect for understanding the relationship between force, mass, and motion.

Learning objectives: Understand how friction affects the motion of objects and Newton's second law | Investigate the relationship between applied force, mass, and acceleration | Predict and verify motion outcomes by adjusting force and friction variables
