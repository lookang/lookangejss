About this Simulation

The Astroblaster Model simulates the dynamics of stacked balls colliding in a box, demonstrating elastic collisions and energy transfer. Learners can adjust box dimensions, initial conditions, and observe velocity and acceleration patterns through interactive graphs while exploring how mass and impact affect collision outcomes.

Learning objectives: Understand energy conservation and momentum transfer in elastic collisions | Analyse velocity and acceleration relationships using real-time graphical data | Investigate how box geometry affects collision dynamics and ball behavior
