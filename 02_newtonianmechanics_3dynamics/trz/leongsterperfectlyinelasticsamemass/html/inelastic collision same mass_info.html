Perfectly Inelastic Same Mass

Tracker Experiment

Pasco Collision Carts with built-in velcros ensure that the cart stick together after collision.
Both carts are appoximately 500 g. Conservation of momentum is applicable, because the force of collision is much higher than the friction. Hence we can assume that there is negligible net external force.

Author: Leong Tze Kwang, Lim Beng Choo
