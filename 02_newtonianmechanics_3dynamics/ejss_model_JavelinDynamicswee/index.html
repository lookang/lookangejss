About this Simulation

This simulation models the flight of a javelin through air, accounting for initial velocity, launch angle, and aerodynamic forces. Learners can adjust parameters like throw speed and angle to investigate how these variables affect flight distance, maximum height, and trajectory shape in realistic conditions.

Learning objectives: Understand how launch angle and velocity influence projectile range | Explore the role of air resistance and aerodynamic drag in athletic performance | Analyze real-world applications of physics principles in sports
