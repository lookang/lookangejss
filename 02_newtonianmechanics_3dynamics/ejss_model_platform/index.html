About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models the motion of a cylinder resting on a horizontally accelerated platform. Learners can adjust platform acceleration, initial conditions, and view parameters to observe how the cylinder responds to changing reference frames, inertial forces, and friction constraints in real-time.

Learning objectives: Understand how objects behave on accelerating platforms and inertial reference frames | Explore the relationship between platform acceleration, friction, and cylinder motion | Apply Newton's laws to predict and verify object dynamics in non-inertial systems
