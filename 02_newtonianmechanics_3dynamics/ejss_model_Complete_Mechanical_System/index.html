About this Simulation

This simulation models a complete mechanical system combining a spring, viscous damper, and two bodies in contact with friction. Learners can adjust spring stiffness, damping coefficient, mass values, and friction parameters to observe how these elements interact and affect system motion, energy loss, and equilibrium behavior.

Learning objectives: Understand how springs, damping, and friction affect coupled mechanical systems | Predict and analyze energy dissipation in damped oscillations | Explore the relationship between system parameters and dynamic response
