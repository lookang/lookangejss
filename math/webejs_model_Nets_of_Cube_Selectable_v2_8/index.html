About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows learners to explore and visualise the nets of cubes—the 2D patterns that fold into 3D cubes. Students can select different net configurations, rotate views, and observe how flat shapes transform into three-dimensional solids, developing spatial reasoning and geometric understanding.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between 2D nets and 3D cube geometry | Develop spatial visualisation and mental rotation skills | Identify and distinguish between valid and invalid cube net patterns
