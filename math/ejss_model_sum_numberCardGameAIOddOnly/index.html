About this Simulation

This interactive card game simulation challenges players to compete against a computer opponent using strategic addition and subtraction. Players draw cards and must manipulate sums to achieve winning conditions based on divisibility rules. The simulation develops numeracy, strategic thinking, and understanding of number properties through engaging gameplay.

Learning objectives: Understand multiples and divisibility by applying modular arithmetic concepts | Develop strategic decision-making by evaluating outcomes of card combinations | Strengthen mental calculation skills through rapid sum and difference operations


Title and author:

Computer Player 1 vs Human Player 2 : Sum and Subtract Number Card Game. To win for Player 2 is Remainder of sum of last 3 cards is multiples of three.
