About this Simulation

This interactive Sudoku simulation allows learners to explore logic puzzles by placing numbers in grids while respecting mathematical constraints. Students can manipulate cell values, observe row and column interactions, and develop systematic problem-solving strategies through hands-on experimentation with Sudoku's deduction rules.

Learning objectives: Understand constraint satisfaction and logical deduction in problem-solving | Develop systematic strategies for eliminating possibilities and finding solutions | Practice spatial reasoning and pattern recognition in mathematical puzzles
