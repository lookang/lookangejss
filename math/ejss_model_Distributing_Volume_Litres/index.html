About this Simulation

This virtual lab allows students to explore how water volume is distributed across multiple containers of different sizes. By manipulating total volume and container capacities, learners investigate litres, remainders, and the relationship between tank levels and bucket or flask sizes in a dynamic, hands-on environment.

Learning objectives: Understand volume measurement in litres and how to distribute quantities across containers | Explore remainders and capacity limits when dividing water volume | Develop problem-solving skills by predicting tank levels and container overflow scenarios
