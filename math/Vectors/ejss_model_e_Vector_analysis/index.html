About this Simulation

This simulation enables learners to explore fundamental vector operations in three-dimensional space. Students can manipulate individual vector components, visualise geometric relationships, and investigate how addition, subtraction, and scalar multiplication behave in 3D environments through interactive real-time feedback.

Learning objectives: Understand vector representation and component notation in 3D coordinate systems | Visualise and perform vector addition, subtraction, and scalar multiplication geometrically | Develop spatial reasoning and interpret vector operations algebraically and graphically
