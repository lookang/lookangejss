About this Simulation

This simulation enables learners to investigate the properties and characteristics of rational and irrational numbers. Students can explore decimal representations, examine terminating and non-terminating patterns, and discover how to distinguish between rational numbers (expressible as fractions p/q) and irrational numbers through interactive manipulation and visualization.

Learning objectives: Distinguish between rational and irrational numbers | Understand decimal representations and their patterns | Express numbers as fractions and identify their mathematical properties
