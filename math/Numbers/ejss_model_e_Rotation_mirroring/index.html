About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows learners to explore symmetry operations including rotation and mirroring transformations. By manipulating geometric shapes and observing how they transform, students develop intuitive understanding of how rotation and reflection preserve geometric properties while changing position and orientation.

Learning objectives: Understand how rotation and mirroring operations transform geometric shapes | Identify invariant properties under symmetry transformations | Apply symmetry operations to analyse and predict geometric outcomes
