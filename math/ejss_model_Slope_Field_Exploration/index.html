About this Simulation

This interactive slope field explorer allows learners to visualize how differential equations behave through dynamic direction fields. By adjusting initial conditions and observing arrow vectors across the coordinate plane, students can discover solution curves and develop intuition for differential equation behavior.

Learning objectives: Understand how slope fields represent solutions to differential equations | Visualize the relationship between direction fields and solution curves | Explore how initial conditions affect solution trajectories in differential systems
