About this Simulation

This shape assembly simulation enables learners to construct and combine 2D geometric shapes dynamically. Students explore spatial relationships by rotating, positioning, and combining shapes to form complex patterns and designs, developing visual-spatial thinking and geometric reasoning skills.

Learning objectives: Understand how basic shapes combine to form composite figures | Develop spatial reasoning and shape manipulation skills | Explore transformations including rotation and translation in geometry
