Fraction Memory Game

Time: 0s

How to Play:

  1. Select a level (1, 2, or 3).
  2. Click the 'START' button to begin the timer and enable card clicking.
  3. Click on two cards to flip them over.
  4. If the fractions on the cards are equivalent (e.g., 1/2 and 2/4), they are a match and will stay face up.
  5. If they are not a match, they will flip back over.
  6. Continue until all pairs are found.
  7. Click 'RESTART' to reset the game at any time or to apply a new level selection.
