About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows students to explore circle geometry and angular relationships dynamically. By adjusting parameters like angle, radius, and center coordinates, learners can visualize how these elements interact and affect circle properties, developing deeper understanding of geometric principles through hands-on experimentation.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between angles and circle geometry | Explore how changing radius and center position affects circular properties | Develop spatial reasoning through interactive geometric visualization
