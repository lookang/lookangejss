About this Simulation

This simulation enables learners to dynamically assemble and manipulate geometric shapes by adjusting dimensions, quantities, and visual properties. Students can construct complex polygonal structures, experiment with shape parameters like base length and height, and explore how individual shapes combine to form larger compositions.

Learning objectives: Understand geometric properties and spatial relationships through hands-on shape assembly | Develop problem-solving skills by experimenting with shape parameters and configurations | Visualise how basic geometric shapes combine to create complex structures
