About this Simulation

This interactive simulation challenges learners to master knight movement patterns on a customisable chessboard. Players strategically move a knight to land exclusively on star-marked squares, optimising the number of valid moves possible. The simulation dynamically visualises board configurations, star placements, and valid knight trajectories.

Learning objectives: Understand L-shaped knight movement geometry and spatial reasoning | Develop strategic planning and constraint-based problem solving | Explore combinatorial optimisation through game-based learning
