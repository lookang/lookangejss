About this Simulation

This interactive magic square simulation challenges learners to fill a 3×3 grid with digits 1 to 9 so that every row, column and diagonal adds up to 15. Students explore number combinations, develop logical reasoning, and discover mathematical patterns through hands-on experimentation in a risk-free digital environment.

Learning objectives: Develop computational thinking and number sense through systematic problem-solving | Understand properties of addition and numerical relationships | Build persistence and analytical skills by testing different digit arrangements
