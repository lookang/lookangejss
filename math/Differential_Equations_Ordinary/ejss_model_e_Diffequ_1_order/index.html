About this Simulation

This simulation enables learners to visualize and solve first-order differential equations interactively. By adjusting initial conditions, step size, and viewing parameters, students explore how solution curves evolve and understand the relationship between differential equations and their graphical representations in the xy-plane.

Learning objectives: Understand the concept of first-order differential equations and their solutions | Visualize slope fields and solution curves for different initial conditions | Explore how numerical methods approximate solutions to differential equations
