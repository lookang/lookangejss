About this Simulation

This virtual lab allows learners to pour water into pails and containers of varying sizes (1, 2, 5, and 10 litres) to understand volume measurement. Students observe how different container capacities hold different amounts of water, developing practical understanding of litres as a standard unit of volume measurement.

Learning objectives: Measure and compare volumes of liquids using litre as a standard unit | Understand the relationship between container capacity and volume | Apply volume measurement skills to real-world pouring and filling scenarios
