About this Simulation

The cube Visualiser is an interactive 3D geometry tool that enables learners to explore cubes from multiple perspectives. Students can rotate, manipulate, and examine cube properties including faces, edges, vertices, and dimensional relationships in real time, deepening their understanding of three-dimensional geometry.

Learning objectives: Understand the structural properties and characteristics of cubes | Develop spatial reasoning and visualization skills through interactive 3D manipulation | Apply geometric knowledge to analyse relationships between cube dimensions and components
