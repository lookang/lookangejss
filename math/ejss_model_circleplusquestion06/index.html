About this Simulation

This simulation enables learners to investigate circle geometry through interactive exploration. Students can manipulate geometric elements, adjust parameters like position and movement, and observe real-time changes to understand circle properties and relationships. The dynamic environment supports experimentation with mathematical concepts in an engaging, visual format.

Learning objectives: Understand circle geometry properties and their relationships | Develop spatial reasoning through interactive manipulation and observation | Connect abstract mathematical concepts to dynamic visual representations
