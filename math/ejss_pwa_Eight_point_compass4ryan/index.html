About this Simulation

This interactive simulation helps students master eight-point compass directions (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW) through coordinate-based navigation challenges. Starting from a green square, learners identify directions and positions, receiving randomized questions and immediate feedback to build directional and spatial literacy skills.

Learning objectives: Identify and name the eight cardinal and intercardinal compass points accurately | Apply directional language and coordinates to locate positions on a grid | Develop spatial reasoning and map-reading confidence through repeated, adaptive practice
