About this Simulation

This interactive Add to Twenty Game allows learners to practice addition by reaching twenty from a customizable starting number. Players develop mental maths strategies, explore number bonds, and strengthen computational fluency through engaging gameplay with adjustable difficulty levels.

Learning objectives: Understand number bonds and parts-whole relationships | Develop efficient mental addition strategies | Build confidence with number fluency and quick calculation
