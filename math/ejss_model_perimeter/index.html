About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows learners to construct shapes by drawing lines and directly observe how perimeter and area change. Students can experiment with different configurations, manipulate line positions in real-time, and develop intuitive understanding of these fundamental geometric properties through hands-on exploration.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between shape boundaries and perimeter measurement | Discover how area changes when shapes are modified | Apply geometric reasoning to solve construction and measurement problems
