About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models a tug of war competition where players roll dice to generate forces and predict outcomes. Learners explore how mathematics governs competition results, test different strategies, and analyse the relationship between random events and mathematical probability in a fun, competitive context.

Learning objectives: Understand how dice outcomes translate into force values and game results | Develop strategic thinking by predicting and comparing winning probabilities | Apply mathematical reasoning to analyse competitive scenarios and fair play rules
