About this Simulation

This simulation allows learners to explore tank-filling scenarios through interactive, real-time visualisation. Students manipulate variables including pail positions and water placement to investigate mathematical relationships between volume, capacity, and fill rates across multiple scenarios.

Learning objectives: Understand volume and capacity relationships through hands-on experimentation | Apply problem-solving strategies to optimise tank-filling sequences | Develop spatial reasoning and mathematical modelling skills through interactive scenario exploration
