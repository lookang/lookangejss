About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows learners to explore line symmetry by selecting different lines of reflection and observing how shapes transform across axes. Students can visualize symmetrical properties, test their understanding of reflection geometry, and experiment with various symmetrical configurations on a coordinate plane.

Learning objectives: Understand how line symmetry reflects shapes across an axis | Identify and verify axes of symmetry in geometric figures | Apply reflection transformations to coordinate points and analyze symmetrical relationships
