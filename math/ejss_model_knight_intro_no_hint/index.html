About this Simulation

This interactive Knight's Move Game challenges learners to plan and execute the maximum number of consecutive knight moves on a customisable board. Players explore chess knight movement patterns, spatial reasoning, and strategic planning by experimenting with different board dimensions and move sequences to optimise their total moves.

Learning objectives: Understand and apply the knight's L-shaped movement rule in chess | Develop strategic thinking by planning multi-move sequences | Explore combinatorial patterns and constraints in spatial problem-solving
