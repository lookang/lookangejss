About this Simulation

Shut the Box is an interactive dice game that challenges players to close numbered tiles by rolling dice and selecting number combinations strategically. Learners explore probability theory, number decomposition, and strategic decision-making in a dynamic, engaging environment that combines chance with tactical gameplay.

Learning objectives: Understand probability and outcomes when rolling multiple dice | Develop number sense by finding equivalent combinations that sum to dice totals | Apply strategic thinking to maximise success in games of chance and skill
