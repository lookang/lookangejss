About this Simulation

This interactive 2-player shape assembly game challenges learners to build designs by combining geometric shapes (A–H) with different areas. Given a base unit shape A, students must construct designs meeting specific target areas using available shapes, developing ratio understanding and spatial reasoning through hands-on exploration and strategic problem-solving.

Learning objectives: Understand and apply ratio concepts by calculating areas of composite shapes | Develop critical thinking through designing solutions that meet geometric constraints | Enhance spatial visualization and mathematical communication in competitive gameplay
