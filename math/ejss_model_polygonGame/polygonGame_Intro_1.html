A  Rhombus By Any Other Name

1. Colour one primary polygon during each turn

2. The die result determines the type of polygon that the player should colour / complete

3. A completion move results in a free turn (a completion move involves forming the desired shape using multiple previously shaded regions to form a larger shape)

4. When making a competion move between 2 shaded regions, the entire new region is counted

5. If a player is not able to colour any shape, the turn moves to the opponent
