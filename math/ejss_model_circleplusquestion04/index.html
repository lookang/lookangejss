About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows students to explore circle geometry concepts through dynamic visualization. Learners can manipulate angles and observe how circular properties change in real-time, developing deeper understanding of angular relationships and circle theorems through hands-on experimentation and visual feedback.

Learning objectives: Understand relationships between angles and circle geometry | Develop spatial reasoning through interactive exploration | Apply circle theorems to solve geometric problems
