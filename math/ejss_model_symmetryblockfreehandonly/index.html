About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows primary students to explore mathematical symmetry using virtual blocks and freehand drawing tools. Learners can create, manipulate, and observe symmetrical patterns in real-time, discovering how reflection, rotation, and line symmetry work through direct hands-on experimentation and visual feedback.

Learning objectives: Identify and create symmetrical shapes and patterns | Understand line symmetry, rotational symmetry, and reflection concepts | Apply symmetry principles to design and problem-solving activities
