## Description

Learn how to play Sun Game, how the buttons work, and how to solve the built-in puzzles step by step.

In this tutorial, viewers will learn:
- how to read the number clues
- how to click edges through active, flagged, and undecided states
- how to use `Teacher Mode`, `Hint`, `Next Step`, `Play Thinking`, and `Solve Now`
- how to spot strong opening moves in Puzzle 1 and Puzzle 2
- why Puzzle 3 and Puzzle 4 are better challenge puzzles

The main goal is not just to get the final answer, but to understand the thinking process behind the puzzle.
