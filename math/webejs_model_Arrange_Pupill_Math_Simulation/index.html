About this Simulation

This simulation lets learners explore spatial arrangement and positioning by placing pupils within different room scenarios. Students manipulate pupil positions using interactive coordinates and examine how arrangement logic works across multiple configurations and viewpoints.

Learning objectives: Understand spatial positioning and coordinate systems in a practical context | Develop logical thinking through scenario-based problem solving | Visualize how objects can be arranged and rearranged in constrained spaces
