About this Simulation

Jack Pot Spin is an interactive simulation that models probability and randomness through spinning mechanics. Learners can visualize how outcomes distribute across multiple trials, observe patterns in random events, and explore concepts like decay, cell-based positioning, and vector visualization to deepen understanding of statistical behavior.

Learning objectives: Understand probability distributions through repeated random trials | Visualize and analyze patterns in chance-based outcomes | Connect abstract probability concepts to dynamic, interactive representations
