About this Simulation

This interactive Add to Twenty game helps learners develop number sense and mental addition skills. Players explore different number combinations that sum to twenty, practising rapid recall and strategic thinking. The simulation provides instant feedback and adjustable difficulty levels to support learners at different stages.

Learning objectives: Recognise and fluently recall number pairs that total twenty | Apply mental maths strategies to solve addition problems quickly | Develop understanding of number composition and number bonds
