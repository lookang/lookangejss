About this Simulation

This simulation immerses learners in real-world mathematical scenarios by navigating multiple locations—home, market, café, grandma's house, and a postbox. Students encounter decisions and challenges that require mathematical thinking, encouraging them to reason aloud and articulate their problem-solving processes. The interactive environment makes abstract concepts concrete and relatable.

Learning objectives: Develop mathematical reasoning and thinking-aloud communication skills | Apply maths to real-world decision-making contexts | Build confidence in explaining mathematical thought processes
