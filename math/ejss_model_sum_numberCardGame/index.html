About this Simulation

This interactive card game challenges two players to strategically sum and subtract number cards. Players must think critically about number combinations while applying divisibility rules. The simulation reinforces arithmetic skills, mathematical reasoning, and game strategy as learners predict outcomes and calculate remainders in real-time gameplay.

Learning objectives: Develop fluency in addition and subtraction through strategic gameplay | Apply divisibility rules and remainder concepts to solve mathematical problems | Enhance logical reasoning and decision-making in competitive mathematical contexts


Title and author:

2 Players Sum and Subtract Number Card Game. To win for Player 2 is Remainder of sum of last 3 cards is multiples of three.
