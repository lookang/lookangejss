About this Simulation

This interactive tangram simulation lets learners explore classic Chinese puzzle geometry while dynamically calculating area and perimeter. Students can manipulate tangram pieces, observe how shape composition affects measurements, and develop spatial reasoning skills through hands-on experimentation with geometric properties.

Learning objectives: Understand the relationship between area and perimeter in composite shapes | Develop spatial visualization and problem-solving skills using tangram pieces | Apply mathematical measurement concepts to interactive geometric exploration
