About this Simulation

This interactive simulation helps learners master eight-point compass directions (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW) using a rotating animal-based approach. As the compass rotates, different animals appear at each cardinal and intercardinal point, allowing learners to practice identifying directions while tracking sequential animal patterns and building spatial reasoning skills.

Learning objectives: Identify and name all eight compass directions accurately | Understand the relationship between cardinal and intercardinal points | Develop spatial awareness and directional literacy through interactive exploration
