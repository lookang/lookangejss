About this Simulation

This interactive gear simulation allows learners to explore the mathematical concept of factors by manipulating cogwheels with different numbers of teeth. By adjusting gear parameters and observing how gears mesh and rotate together, students discover which numbers divide evenly into others and develop a deeper understanding of factors and divisibility through hands-on visual exploration.

Learning objectives: Identify and understand factors of whole numbers through gear tooth counts | Visualize the relationship between divisibility and gear meshing | Develop number sense by exploring which gear combinations work together smoothly
