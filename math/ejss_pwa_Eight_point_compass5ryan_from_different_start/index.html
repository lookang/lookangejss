About this Simulation

This interactive simulation helps students master eight-point compass directions (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW) through engaging animal-based scenarios. Learners identify directions from randomised starting positions, developing spatial awareness and directional literacy essential for maps, coordinates, and navigation skills in primary mathematics.

Learning objectives: Identify and name all eight compass points accurately | Determine relative directions from a given starting position | Apply directional reasoning to solve coordinate and spatial problems
