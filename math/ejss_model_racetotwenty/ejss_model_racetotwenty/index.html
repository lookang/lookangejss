About this Simulation

The Add to Twenty Game is an interactive maths simulation where learners practise combining numbers to reach a target sum of twenty. Players explore different number pairs and strategic choices while receiving real-time feedback and hints to guide their learning. This engaging game builds fluency with number bonds and mental arithmetic skills.

Learning objectives: Develop understanding of number combinations that sum to twenty | Practise mental addition and strategic number selection | Build confidence and fluency in foundational arithmetic skills
