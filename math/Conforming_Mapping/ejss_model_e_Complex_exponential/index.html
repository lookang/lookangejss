About this Simulation

This simulation enables students to visualize conformal mappings and complex exponential functions in real time. By adjusting domain parameters and viewing transformations, learners explore how complex functions map regions while preserving local angles. The interactive tool demonstrates fundamental concepts in complex analysis through direct visual feedback.

Learning objectives: Understand how conformal mappings preserve angles between curves | Investigate the properties of the complex exponential function and its geometric effects | Develop intuition for complex function behaviour through interactive exploration and real-time visualization
