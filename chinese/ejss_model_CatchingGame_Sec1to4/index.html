About this Simulation

This interactive catching game helps learners master correct Chinese phrases through engaging gameplay. Students catch falling phrases, identify correct expressions, and receive immediate feedback on their language choices. The simulation adjusts difficulty by varying the number of rounds and answer options, allowing customizable practice sessions that build confidence and fluency in phrase recognition.

Learning objectives: Identify and select correct Chinese phrases in context | Build vocabulary recognition and retention through interactive practice | Develop rapid language processing skills under timed conditions
