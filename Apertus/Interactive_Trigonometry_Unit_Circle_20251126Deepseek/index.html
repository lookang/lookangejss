Practice Problems

Find the exact value of sin(30°).

Step 1: Identify the angle on the unit circle.

The unit circle helps visualize trigonometric values for any angle.

Step 2: Find the y-coordinate of the point on the unit circle.

On the unit circle, sin(θ) equals the y-coordinate of the point.

Step 3: For 30°, the coordinates are (√3/2, 1/2).

These values come from the special 30-60-90 right triangle.

Step 4: Therefore, sin(30°) = 1/2.
