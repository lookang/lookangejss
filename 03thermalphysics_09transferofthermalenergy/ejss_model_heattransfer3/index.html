About this Simulation

This simulation models how thermal energy transfers between objects and materials. Learners can adjust object sizes, positions, and temperatures to observe heat flow in action, discovering how conduction, convection, and radiation work in everyday situations.

Learning objectives: Understand the three main mechanisms of heat transfer | Predict how temperature differences affect energy movement | Explore variables that influence thermal energy flow between objects
