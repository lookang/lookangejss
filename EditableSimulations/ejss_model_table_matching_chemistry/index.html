About this Simulation

This interactive matching game helps learners classify substances by dragging and pairing elements, compounds, and mixtures into corresponding columns. Students explore the fundamental differences between these three categories through visual pattern recognition and hands-on matching activities, reinforcing core chemistry concepts in an engaging, game-based format.

Learning objectives: Distinguish between elements, compounds, and mixtures based on their properties | Develop classification and categorisation skills through interactive matching | Apply knowledge of substance composition to correctly organise chemical examples
