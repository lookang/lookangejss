About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models blood alcohol concentration (BAC) dynamics by allowing learners to adjust variables such as body mass, gender, and alcohol consumption quantity. Students can visualize how physiological factors influence BAC levels in real-time, exploring the relationship between alcohol intake and metabolic processes.

Learning objectives: Understand how body composition and gender affect alcohol metabolism and BAC levels | Quantitatively model the relationship between alcohol consumption variables and concentration in the bloodstream | Apply mathematical reasoning to interpret health and safety implications of blood alcohol concentration
