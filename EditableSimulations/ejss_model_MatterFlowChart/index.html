About this Simulation

FlowChart is an interactive visualization tool that enables learners to construct and explore hierarchical flowcharts of matter, properties, and relationships. Students can build custom diagrams to classify states of matter, organize scientific concepts, and develop visual understanding of complex systems through hands-on creation and exploration.

Learning objectives: Create and manipulate flowcharts to organize and classify scientific information | Develop visual literacy and concept mapping skills in science | Understand hierarchical relationships and categorical organization of matter and properties
