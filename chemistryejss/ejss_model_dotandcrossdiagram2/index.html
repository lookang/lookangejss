About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows students to construct and visualize polyatomic ion structures using dot and cross diagrams. Learners can explore how electrons are distributed, identify bonding patterns, and understand the relationship between electron arrangement and ion charge through hands-on experimentation.

Learning objectives: Represent electron distribution in polyatomic ions using dot and cross notation | Identify covalent bonds and lone pairs in complex ion structures | Understand how valence electrons determine ion properties and stability
