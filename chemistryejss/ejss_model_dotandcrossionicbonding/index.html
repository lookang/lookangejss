About this Simulation

This interactive simulation allows students to explore ionic bonding through dot and cross diagrams. Learners can visualise how electrons transfer between atoms, observe the formation of ions, and understand the electrostatic attractions that create ionic compounds. The tool supports hands-on experimentation with different elements and bonding scenarios.

Learning objectives: Understand electron transfer and the formation of cations and anions | Interpret and construct dot and cross diagrams to represent ionic bonding | Explain how electrostatic forces hold ionic compounds together
