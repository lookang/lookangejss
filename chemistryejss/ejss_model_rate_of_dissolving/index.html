About this Simulation

This interactive simulation models the dissolving process, allowing learners to investigate how different variables affect dissolution rates. Students can manipulate conditions such as temperature, surface area, and stirring intensity to observe real-time changes in how quickly substances dissolve in solution.

Learning objectives: Understand the factors that influence the rate of dissolving | Predict how temperature and surface area changes affect dissolution speed | Apply observations to explain particle behavior during the dissolving process
