About this Simulation

This interactive dot and cross diagram simulation lets students visualise how ionic bonds form through electron transfer between atoms. Learners can manipulate electron positions, track valence electrons, and observe bonding patterns in real time. The simulation includes teacher mode for classroom demonstrations and instant feedback to guide learning.

Learning objectives: Understand electron transfer and ionic bond formation | Represent ionic bonding using dot and cross notation | Identify patterns in electron loss and gain between atoms
