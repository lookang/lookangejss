How to Play

1. Select a cation (positive ion) from the left panel.

2. Select an anion (negative ion) from the right panel.

3. Click "Form Bond" to create an ionic compound.

4. The game will check if your compound is valid based on the charges.

5. Earn points for creating valid compounds!

About Ionic Bonding

Ionic bonding occurs between metals (which form positive ions) and non-metals (which form negative ions). The ions must have balanced charges to form a stable compound.
